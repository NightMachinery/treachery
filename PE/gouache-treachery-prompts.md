# Gouache Treachery image prompts

Prompts are handcrafted from `PE/image-gouache-1-template.md`. Common constraints for each generated image: gallery-grade modern editorial gouache, 7:10 portrait requested as 1050x1500, full bleed, no text/letters/numbers/logos/watermarks/borders, calm lower 20% for later card label, main subject in the central safe zone.
